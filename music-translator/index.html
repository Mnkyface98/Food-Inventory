Carnatic ↔ Western Note Translator

Convert Carnatic swaras (S R G M P D N) to Western note names, and back — so a phrase played on veena, violin or voice can be read out on piano, guitar or bass.

Sa is the movable "do" of Carnatic music — pick whatever note your instrument's Sa is tuned to.

Carnatic → Western

Enter swaras separated by spaces, e.g. S R2 G3 M1 P D2 N3 S'. Use a trailing ' for the upper octave (tara sthayi) and , for the lower octave (mandra sthayi). A bare letter with no number (e.g. R) is read as its natural-scale variant.

Western → Carnatic

Enter notes separated by spaces, e.g. C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5. Sharps use #, flats use b. Octave number is optional (defaults to the tonic's octave).

Swarasthana chart

The 16 Carnatic swara names collapse onto 12 semitones (like Western chromatic notes) — R3/G2 and D3/N2 are enharmonic pairs.

This is a note-name translator, not a full raga/gamaka transcriber — it maps individual swara pitches to their Western equivalents relative to your chosen Sa. Ornamentation (gamakas) and microtonal shading used in Carnatic performance aren't represented in Western 12-tone notation.
